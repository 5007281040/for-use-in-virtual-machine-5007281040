Another very interesting find. Changing from the default to a round robin policy has interesting effects. 
sudo chrt -p -r 1 <pid> caused the context switching in the watch command, watch -n.5 grep ctxt /proc/<pid>/status
to grow at one at a time. this was also the same effect I saw for a FIFO policy. Both the commands had priority 1
sudo chrt -p -r 1 <pid>
sudo chrt -p -f 1 <pid>

then, another for a priority of 10.
sudo chrt -p -r 10 <pid>
sudo chrt -p -f 10 <pid>

hmm, another very interesting observation. Turning both of their priorities to 10 made the amount of context switching
non-linear. Both policies had an increase of 1 context switch per half second, here it seems to be anywhere from 1-3 switches.
